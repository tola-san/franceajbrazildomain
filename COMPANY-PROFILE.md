# France AJ Alimentaire And Food LTDA — Company Profile

*Delivering Quality from Brazil to the World*

---

## Who We Are

France AJ Alimentaire And Food LTDA is a Brazil-based B2B wholesale food export
company and a proud subsidiary of the **France AJ Group**. From our base in
São Paulo, we serve as a reliable link between Brazil's rich agricultural
production and global markets across Asia, Africa, Europe, and the Middle East.

We specialize in the export of high-quality food commodities — grains, oils,
sugar, and frozen HALAL-certified meats — that meet the highest international
standards for safety, consistency, and freshness. With more than three decades
of experience in the food trade industry, our team understands the complexities
of global sourcing, compliance, and logistics, ensuring every shipment is
delivered efficiently, on time, and in perfect condition — whether it's a single
container or a full-scale recurring supply contract.

## Our Mission

To provide global partners with food products that are not only safe and
competitively priced, but ethically sourced and sustainably delivered — rooted
in quality, driven by values, and focused on our customers' business success.

## Our Core Offerings

We provide a broad portfolio of essential food products tailored to the needs of
importers, distributors, and manufacturers worldwide.

- **Frozen Proteins — Chicken, Beef, Lamb & Seafood.** Premium frozen meats
  sourced from certified Brazilian producers, offering exceptional quality and
  full HALAL compliance for international markets.
- **Sugar — ICUMSA 45, 150, 600–1200.** Top-grade Brazilian sugar in refined and
  raw forms to meet diverse market needs, from industrial use to retail packaging.
- **Corn — GMO, Non-GMO & Feed Grade.** Premium yellow and white corn, suitable
  for both human consumption and animal feed applications.
- **Soybeans — GMO, Non-GMO & Feed Grade.** High-quality soybeans from Brazil's
  fertile regions, available in food-grade, oil-extraction, and animal-feed grades.
- **Vegetable Oils — Soybean & Sunflower (Crude & Refined).** Tailored for
  cooking, manufacturing, and industrial needs.
